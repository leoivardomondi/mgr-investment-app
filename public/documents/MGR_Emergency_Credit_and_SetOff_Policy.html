EMERGENCY CREDIT POLICY

Member 90% Borrowing Limits, Interest Schedule & Mandatory Right of Set-Off
Policy Ref: MGR/POL/CREDIT-01 Authority: Constitution Clause 10.5 Borrowing Limit: 90% Shares Rate: 10% / Month
"Pursuant to Clause 10.5 of the MGR Club Constitution, this policy establishes the formal terms, security thresholds, monthly interest rate, and mandatory set-off mechanisms governing emergency short-term borrowing extended to Members."

1.0 Constitutional Mandate and Purpose

1.1 MGR CLUB may, subject to liquidity availability in treasury accounts, extend short-term emergency financial assistance to Members in good standing.
1.2 The facility exists solely for genuine emergency needs and operates strictly within mutual security parameters to protect group solvency.

2.0 Eligibility & Credit Qualifications

2.1 A Member qualifies for an emergency credit advance if he or she:
  • holds active, fully paid-up membership status with no unresolved arrears;
  • maintains sufficient qualifying share capital and member savings to secure the requested principal; and
  • has satisfactorily discharged any prior emergency borrowings.
3.0 Borrowing Limit, Collateral & Set-Off Guarantee
3.1 Maximum Loan Limit: A Member may borrow an amount not exceeding ninety per cent (90%) of the net shares, savings, or capital contributions standing to his or her credit on the application date.
3.2 Collateral Pledge: The Member's shares and capital balance serve as absolute security. The borrowing Member is legally barred from withdrawing or transferring pledged shares until full discharge.
3.3 Mandatory Right of Set-Off: MGR CLUB holds an unconditional right of set-off under Clause 10.3 to deduct outstanding principal and accrued interest from the Member's capital account upon default, death, or exit.

4.0 Financial Terms, Interest & Repayment

4.1 Interest Rate: All emergency loans attract interest at a flat rate of ten per cent (10%) per month on the principal advanced.
4.2 Repayment Period: The total repayable sum (principal plus interest) must be settled in full within thirty (30) calendar days from disbursement.
4.3 Default Sanctions: Failure to settle within 30 days immediately accelerates the loan, empowers MGR CLUB to execute recovery from shares without further notice, and freezes subsequent borrowing privileges.
