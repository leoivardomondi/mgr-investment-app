YIELD PRORATING POLICY

Holding-Period Weighted Allocations, Post-Period Capital & Unanimous Ratification
Policy Ref: MGR/POL/MMF-DIST-01 Authority: Constitution Clause 9.4 Approval Gate: 100% Unanimous Method: Holding-Period Weighted
"This policy operationalizes Clause 9.4 of the MGR Club Constitution, establishing the mathematical formula, holding-period prorating, post-period capital protection, and unanimous member ratification mandate governing interest distributions."

1.0 The Unanimous Member Ratification Mandate

1.1 No financial action crediting or debiting investment interest to individual member accounts may be executed without the prior unanimous (100%) affirmative ratification of all active members.

2.0 Holding-Period Weighted Allocation Formula

2.1 Investment interest earned from CMA-regulated funds (Mansa-X Special Fund and Etica Capital MMF) shall be attributed based on eligible capital weighted by active days invested during the period.
2.2 Exclusion of Post-Period Capital: Capital received after the close of an accounting quarter (e.g. Leoivard Omondi Ongule MGR-011 depositing in late October 2026, after Q3 closure on 30/09/2026) is strictly excluded from past quarter dividends, receiving KES 0.00 for Q3 and preserving 100% of his KES 17,000 principal for Q4.
2.3 Protection of Deceased Estates: Funds of deceased members that remained active throughout the period (e.g. Late Frederick Ongule Seunda MGR-007) accrue their full pro-rata share into the estate escrow account.
