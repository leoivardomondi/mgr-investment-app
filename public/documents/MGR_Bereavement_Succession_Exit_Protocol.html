BEREAVEMENT SUCCESSION PROTOCOL

Estate Par Value Share Acquisition, Family Settlement & Beneficiary Escrow
Protocol Ref: MGR/POL/BEREAVEMENT-01 Authority: Constitution Clause 10.7 Valuation: 1:1 Par Value Family Payout: 100% Cash
"This protocol codifies MGR CLUB's compassionate bereavement succession procedure, ensuring that when a member passes away, their terminal share capital and accrued earnings are settled at 1:1 par value to the family beneficiary without breaching fund lock-in commitments."

1.0 Constitutional Mandate & Core Principles

1.1 In the event of the death or permanent incapacity of a Member, MGR CLUB's fiduciary duty is to safeguard the deceased member's family entitlements while preserving the investment continuity of the club.
1.2 Under Clause 10.7, all terminal benefits accrue to the nominated legal personal representative or next of kin in accordance with Kenyan succession law.
2.0 Internal 1:1 Par Value Share Buyout Mechanism
2.1 Lock-in Preservation: Because Club capital in Mansa-X Special Fund and Etica Capital MMF is bound by minimum holding cycles, external liquidations would incur penalties.
2.2 Internal Acquisition: Active surviving members are given primary option to purchase the estate's shares at exact 1:1 par value (KES 1.00 per share).
2.3 100% Cash Release: The purchase consideration contributed by buying members is credited directly to the Beneficiary Escrow Account and disbursed to the family.
