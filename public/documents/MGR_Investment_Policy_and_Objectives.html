MGR CLUB INVESTMENT POLICY

Strategic Objectives, SMART Performance Targets & Risk Oversight Charter
Charter Ref: MGR/IPS/2026/V1 Fiduciary Standard: Prudent Investor Rule Review Cycle: Quarterly & Annual Effective: October 2026
"This Investment Policy Statement and Strategic Objectives Charter provides the binding operational framework for translating MGR CLUB's Vision and Mission into disciplined capital allocation, rigorous risk oversight, and sustainable generational prosperity."

3.0 Vision, Mission and Strategic Mandate

3.1 Vision Statement
"Financial strength, sustainable growth, and enduring prosperity for current and future generations."
3.2 Mission Statement
"Creating and preserving wealth through disciplined investment management, informed decision-making, prudent risk oversight, and an unwavering commitment to integrity, transparency, and accountability."

4.0 Strategic Objectives

To achieve its Vision and fulfil its Mission, the LLP/Club shall pursue the following ten (10) strategic objectives:

4.1 Grow and Preserve Capital: Achieve sustainable growth in the LLP's/Club's investment portfolio while safeguarding capital against undue risk and market volatility.
4.2 Maintain a Diversified Investment Portfolio: Invest across a broad range of asset classes, sectors, and geographical markets to enhance returns and reduce concentration risk.
4.3 Generate Competitive Risk-Adjusted Returns: Consistently seek to outperform relevant market benchmarks through disciplined investment selection and effective portfolio management.
4.4 Apply Rigorous Investment Research: Ensure that all investment decisions are supported by comprehensive financial analysis, market intelligence, and appropriate due diligence.
4.5 Implement Effective Risk Management: Identify, assess, monitor, and mitigate investment risks to protect assets and promote long-term financial sustainability.
4.6 Ensure Liquidity and Financial Stability: Maintain adequate liquidity to meet operational requirements, investment commitments, and unforeseen financial obligations.
4.7 Promote Ethical and Responsible Investing: Uphold the highest standards of integrity, transparency, accountability, and regulatory compliance in all investment activities.
4.8 Enhance Portfolio Performance Through Continuous Review: Regularly evaluate portfolio performance and adjust investment strategies in response to changing market conditions and emerging opportunities.
4.9 Preserve Wealth for Future Generations: Build and sustain a long-term financial legacy through prudent stewardship and responsible investment practices.
4.10 Strengthen Governance and Compliance: Ensure that all investment activities comply with applicable laws, regulations, partnership agreements, and internal policies.

5.0 SMART Objectives & Measurable Benchmarks

The LLP/Club shall pursue the following six (6) Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound (SMART) objectives in support of its strategic goals:

5.1 Portfolio Growth ≥ 10.0% p.a.

Achieve an average annual portfolio return of not less than 10% over rolling five-year periods, subject to prevailing macroeconomic conditions.

5.2 Portfolio Diversification ≤ 20% Cap

Maintain portfolio diversification such that no single investment exceeds 20% of the total portfolio value, unless explicitly approved by the Partners/Members.

5.3 Due Diligence 100% Review

Conduct and document formal due diligence reviews for 100% of proposed investments before approval and capital allocation.

5.4 Performance Monitoring Quarterly Formal

Undertake formal portfolio performance and risk exposure reviews on a quarterly basis and report findings directly to the Partners/Members.

5.5 Liquidity Management ≥ 6 Months Buffer

Maintain a minimum liquidity reserve equivalent to at least six (6) months of projected operational commitments and emergency cash calls.

5.6 Compliance & Reporting 100% Annual Audit

Achieve full compliance with all statutory and regulatory requirements, and complete comprehensive annual compliance reviews prior to AGM.

6.0 IPS Formulation, Research Benchmarking & Three-Tier Ratification

6.1 Three-Tier Governance Protocol: Every Investment Policy Statement (I.P.S.), asset class addition, and strategic rebalancing resolution in MGR CLUB shall strictly follow a structured three-tier workflow:
  • Tier 1 — Research & Peer Benchmarking: Led by the Investment Research Officer / Analyst (Dr. Joash Migosi PhD and desk). The Research Officer conducts comprehensive market intelligence, risk-return profiling, and peer note comparisons with external Chama leaders, investment analysts, and capital market practitioners before floating any proposal.
  • Tier 2 — Executive Committee Deliberation: The drafted policy and risk-reward metrics are formally reviewed, stress-tested, and shaped into binding executive resolutions by the Executive Committee.
  • Tier 3 — General Membership Democratic Ratification: The final IPS framework is tabled before the General Membership and must be democratically ratified before any capital commitment or portfolio restructuring takes effect.
6.2 Alignment with Fiduciary Duty: These objectives provide a transparent framework translating the Club's Vision and Mission into measurable, auditable actions, ensuring strict adherence to the Prudent Investor standard.

7.0 Tactical Asset Allocation, Yield Dynamics & Banking Operations

7.1 Dynamic Yield Monitoring & Agile Portfolio Switching

Mansa-X Special Fund (Standard Investment Bank): Yields in Mansa-X are dynamic and fluctuate according to global multi-asset opportunities and market conditions. While Mansa-X currently provides the most competitive returns in the market (~18%–20% p.a.), the Investment Research Officer and Executive Committee shall continuously monitor performance. In the event of yield compression or shifting risk profiles, the Club shall swiftly compare alternative portfolios to execute timely, agile reallocation decisions.

Etica Capital Money Market Fund: The benchmark figure of 11.08% – 12.50% p.a. reflects prevailing market conditions and is likewise subject to Central Bank of Kenya (CBK) rate changes and money market fluctuations.

7.2 Current Tactical Capital Reallocation (KES 200,000/= Transfer)

Pursuant to active treasury optimization, as soon as Etica Capital MMF processes liquidity access / redemption approvals, MGR CLUB shall execute a tactical transfer of KES 200,000.00 from Etica Capital MMF into the Mansa-X Special Fund account to capture superior competitive compounding returns for all active Members.

7.3 Designated Operating Bank: Equity Bank Kenya (Chama Joint Account)

MGR CLUB maintains its primary commercial clearing and operational bank account with Equity Bank Kenya. Equity Bank was deliberately selected by the Executive Committee as the most flexible financial institution for opening and administering operational Chama Joint Bank Accounts without premature demand for formal LLP registration certificates, while offering the lowest ledger and account maintenance fees in the Kenyan commercial banking sector.

8.0 Strategic Member Research Horizons (Six Priority Portfolios)

To foster shared financial literacy and strategic preparedness, all Members of MGR CLUB are formally encouraged to conduct wide research and comparative analysis across six (6) approved asset classes:

1. Fixed Income Funds (FIFs) High Yield

Institutional corporate debt funds, secured commercial paper, and high-yield private credit yielding predictable periodic coupons above standard deposits.

2. Derivatives (NEXT Market) Hedging & Yield

Nairobi Securities Exchange Derivatives Market (NEXT) equity index futures, single-stock futures, and risk hedging mechanisms against downside market swings.

3. Real Estate Trusts (REITs) ≥ 80% Payout

CMA-regulated Income REITs (I-REITs like ILAM Fahari) and Development REITs (D-REITs like Acorn), granting fractional access to premium real estate.

4. Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) Liquid Hedge

Exchange-listed physical commodity trackers (e.g. Absa NewGold ETF on the NSE) offering physical bullion backing and a hedge against local currency volatility.

5. Gov Securities, T-Bills/Bonds & IFs Tax-Free Sovereign

Central Bank of Kenya Treasury Bills (91/182/364-day), Treasury Bonds, and especially Infrastructure Funds / Bonds (IFs/IFBs) delivering 100% tax-free coupon yields with zero default risk.

6. NSE Equities (Equities Desk) Capital Growth

Blue-chip equities on the Nairobi Securities Exchange (Safaricom, Equity Group, KCB, EABL, StanChart) for long-term capital compounding and franked dividends.
